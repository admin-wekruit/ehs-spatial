# r5b vocabulary: the selection rule (written 2026-09-29 before any comparison result was read)

The default wave-2 word source is the VLM-free source with the most held-out items typed in the right family, summed over
the three videos (runs/mvp2-results/identity-heldout; the r5b scorer, where an 'other:' label counts by the family its thing
belongs to; the cards' names under the r5b family rule), ties broken by clean delivered objects covered (the instances
scorer), then by the time to cards v3. YOLOE-26 prompt-free is AGPL-3.0: it could only be the internal profile's default;
the commercial default would then be the next source. 'qwen' stays an opt-in comparison whatever it scores.
