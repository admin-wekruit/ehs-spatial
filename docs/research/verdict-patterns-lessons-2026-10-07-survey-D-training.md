# End-to-end 3D/4D scene understanding + rule comparison vs. scene graphs + rules — literature survey (as of 2026-10-07)

**Short answer.** Yes, people are pre-/post-training models to do spatial understanding end-to-end, and a few 2025-26 papers try "learned compliance". None of them reaches cm-level metric reliability, none produces an audit trail by construction, and the two best-performing 2026 results on spatial benchmarks come from *putting an explicit 3D scene graph plus geometric tools under an LLM*, not from end-to-end training. The mature pattern is: foundation models emit symbols with uncertainty; symbolic rules judge; explanation is the evidence chain. Details and numbers below.

## 1. 3D vision-language models (pretraining / instruction tuning)

| Model (date) | Trained on | Reported | Limits |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3D-LLM (Jul 2023) | ~300k 3D-text pairs; multi-view 2D features lifted to 3D; 2D VLM backbone | ScanQA ~+9% BLEU-1 (val) over the ScanQA baseline ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2307.12981)) | captioning-style metrics; no metric relations |
| LEO (Nov 2023, ICML 2024) | 3D VL alignment then VLA tuning | ScanQA CIDEr 101.4, SQA3D EM 50.0 (secondary source, [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.12871)) | object-centric tokens from GT/segmented objects |
| SceneVerse / GPS (Jan 2024, ECCV 2024) | 68K indoor scenes, 2.5M VL pairs, mostly template text generated *from scene graphs* ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.09340)) | SOTA on grounding/QA at the time | the "pretraining data" is itself scene-graph-derived — relations are the supervision, not the output |
| Chat-Scene (NeurIPS 2024) | object-identifier tokens over detected proposals | ScanQA CIDEr 87.7, SQA3D EM 54.6, ScanRefer Acc@0.5 50.2 ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.08168)) | depends on an upstream instance segmenter |
| Grounded 3D-LLM (May 2024) | 1M auto-curated phrase-region pairs, CLASP contrastive pretraining ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.10370)) | unified grounding/QA | ScanNet-only |
| LLaVA-3D (Sep 2024, ICCV 2025) | 3D patches on LLaVA-Video-7B, 859k samples | SQA3D 55.6, ScanQA EM 27.0 ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.18125)) | |
| Video-3D LLM (Dec 2024, CVPR 2025) | LLaVA-Video-7B + 3D position encoding from RGB-D, 223k samples | ScanRefer 58.1 Acc@0.25, SQA3D 58.6 EM, ScanQA 30.1 EM ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.00493)) | needs depth/poses |
| VLM-3R (May 2025, CVPR 2026) | CUT3R geometry tokens, LoRA, 207k QA | VSI-Bench 60.9 avg; abs-distance 70.2, route 80.5 ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.20279)) | in-domain ScanNet-style scenes; multiple-choice/relative metrics |
| Spatial-MLLM (May 2025, NeurIPS 2025) | Qwen2.5-VL-3B + VGGT encoder; 120k QA from ScanNet; SFT + GRPO | VSI-Bench 48.4 (count 65.3, rel-dir 46.2); SQA3D 55.9 ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.23747)) | 4B; same domain |

**Benchmark validity (the important caveat).** ACL Findings 2025 "Revisiting 3D LLM Benchmarks" shows "2D-cheating": a VLM fed rendered point-cloud images beats 3D-LLM SOTA on several benchmarks ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.08503)). ICLR 2026 "Do 3D LLMs really understand 3D spatial relationships?" shows a *text-only* fine-tuned LM matches or beats 3D-LLMs on SQA3D with no 3D input, and proposes Real-3DQA ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.23523)). So ScanQA/SQA3D gains do not certify relation understanding.

**Relations are the weak link (3DSSG).** 3DSSG = 1,553 3RScan scenes, 160 object classes, 26 predicates. With predicted object labels (SGCls), relation recall is poor: SGFN R@50 28.9 / mR@50 20.8; VL-SAT 31.0 / 29.9; the Nov 2025 edge-centric model 30.1 / 32.3 (PredCls mR@50 68.5). The authors call out systematic confusion among "hanging on / attached to / supported by" ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.15288)). Open-vocabulary variants (Open3DSG, [CVPR 2024](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.12259)) widen vocabulary, not accuracy. Conclusion: *learned* predicates are unreliable; *computed* geometric predicates (distance, support, containment from reconstruction) are not.

## 2. Spatial-reasoning post-training of VLMs

- **SpatialVLM** (Jan 2024, CVPR 2024): 2B synthetic spatial VQA from 10M images via depth + detection + 3D lifting. Quantitative questions: 37.2% of answers within [0.5x, 2x] of ground truth vs 0.0% for GPT-4V; GPT-4V emits a valid number only 1% of the time ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.12168)). "Within 2x" is the success criterion — not cm.
- **SpatialRGPT** (Jun 2024, NeurIPS 2024): region prompts + depth plug-in; 8.7M spatial facts over 5M regions generated from 3D scene graphs. SpatialRGPT-Bench success = within ±25%: direct distance 35.1% (GPT-4V 29.7%), horizontal 59.0% (25.4%), vertical 53.8% (33.0%), direction 95.3% (43.9%); qualitative relations 91.8% vs 58.1% ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.01584)). Qualitative relations are solved; metric distance is roughly a coin flip at ±25%.
- **Thinking in Space / VSI-Bench** (Dec 2024, CVPR 2025 oral): 5k+ QA on 288 videos. Humans 79%; Gemini-1.5 Pro 45.4%; GPT-4o 34.0%; best open 40.9%. Gemini per task: abs distance 30.9, route plan 36.0, rel direction 46.3, count 56.2, object size 64.1. Near chance on abs distance, route plan, relative direction; 71% of errors attributed to spatial reasoning (relational, egocentric-allocentric); CoT/self-consistency do not help; emitting an explicit cognitive map adds +10 pts on relative distance ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.14171)).
- **Multi-SpatialMLLM** (May 2025, CVPR 2026): MultiSPA 27M samples from 1.1M images (ScanNet, Aria DT, Panoptic Studio, TAPVid3D); InternVL2-8B LoRA. 56.1% vs GPT-4o 28.9% on its benchmark; qualitative tasks 80-90%, quantitative still low (camera-motion vector 18%); mostly two-view ([HF paper page](https://huggingface.co/papers/2505.17015)).
- **RLVR family**: SpaceR (Apr 2025, SpaceR-151k, GRPO + map imagination, +11.6% over GPT-4o on VSI-Bench, [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.01805)); ViLaSR (NeurIPS 2025, drawing ops, VSI-Bench 45.4 at 7B, [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.09965)); SpatialLadder (ICLR 2026, 3B, perception→understanding→RLVR, VSI-Bench 45.7, [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.08531)); SVQA-R1 (view-consistency reward, +30% over SFT on Q-Spatial++, [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.01371)); Smooth Operator (Jan 2026) notes GRPO treats numeric near-misses as failures and adds a smooth numeric reward on Numerical3D-50k ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.07695)).
- **Scaling**: Cambrian-S (Nov 2025): VSI-590K, 7B reaches 67.5% VSI-Bench, but VSI-Super drops 38.3%→6.0% from 10- to 60-minute video ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.04670)). Critique two weeks later: a bag-of-words SigLIP "NoSense" baseline gets 95% on VSI-Super-Recall and Cambrian-S falls 42%→0% when the video is concatenated with itself ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.16655)). SenseNova-SI (Nov 2025, CVPR 2026): 8M samples, VSI-Bench 68.8, and explicitly discusses overfitting and language shortcuts ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.13719)). EASI (Aug 2025): GPT-5 "still falls short of human performance significantly" ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.13142)). ReVSI (ICML 2026) re-annotated 381 scenes because reconstruction artifacts corrupt size answers and sparse frame sampling makes many questions unanswerable ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.24300)).
- **The 2026 counter-result**: RieMind (Mar 2026) puts an LLM over an explicit 3D scene graph with geometric tools (dimensions, distances, poses), no fine-tuning, and reports up to +16% over prior work and +33-50% over base VLMs on VSI-Bench static ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.15386)). Explicit geometry beats end-to-end.
- **Pointing / boxes**: Molmo (Sep 2024): PixMo-Points 2.3M human points on 223k images; point-then-count gives CountBenchQA 91.2% at 72B ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.17146)). Gemini Robotics / ERQA (Mar 2025): 400 questions; Gemini 2.0 Pro 48.3% (54.8% with CoT) ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.20020)). Gemini Robotics-ER 1.5 (25 Sep 2025): points, 2D boxes, "SOTA on 15 benchmarks"; the blog gives charts but no per-benchmark numbers ([Google blog](https://developers.googleblog.com/building-the-next-generation-of-physical-agents-with-gemini-robotics-er-15/)); secondary figures (Point-Bench ~52.6% aggregate, monocular 3D-box AP@15 48.3%) are **unverified**. ER 1.6 (14 Apr 2026): pointing "avoids hallucinating absent objects"; gauge/sight-glass reading 86%→93% with agentic vision ([DeepMind](https://deepmind.google/blog/gemini-robotics-er-1-6/)).

## 3. 4D / temporal

- **Dynamic / 4D scene graphs (robotics, symbolic)**: Hydra (RSS 2022) real-time hierarchical 3DSG ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2201.13360)); Khronos (RSS 2024) spatio-temporal metric-semantic SLAM: fast short-term window + factor-graph long-term change reasoning ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.13817)); DovSG (Oct 2024) dynamic open-vocab 3DSG for long-horizon manipulation ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.11989)); ST-OVSG (Sep 2025) 74% node accuracy on Replica ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.23107)); LOST-3DSG (Jan 2026) ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.02905)); Aion (Dec 2025, ICRA 2026) attaches Maps-of-Dynamics/FreMEn flow models to place nodes for time-aware planning ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.11903)); WSGG / ActionGenome4D (Mar 2026) predicts a per-timestamp world-frame graph including occluded objects ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.13185)). All are built for navigation/manipulation; none is used for compliance.
- **Learned video scene graphs**: Action Genome SGDET (full pipeline) best is DiffVsgg (CVPR 2025) R@10/20/50 = 32.8/39.9/45.5 ([CVPR](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2025/papers/Chen_DiffVsgg_Diffusion-Driven_Online_Video_Scene_Graph_Generation_CVPR_2025_paper.pdf)); STRE mR@20 SGDET 32.4 ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.14524)). PVSG (CVPR 2023, 400 videos) best baseline R@20 3.88%, "roughly one of every 25 ground-truth triplets" recalled ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.17058)). Learned temporal predicates are nowhere near verdict-grade.
- **World models**: V-JEPA 2 (Jun 2025): 1M+ hours video, SSv2 77.3, action-conditioned variant from 62h Droid, zero-shot pick/place ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.09985)). NVIDIA Cosmos (Jan 2025): 20M hours, 9,000T tokens, diffusion + AR generators ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.03575)). Genie 3 (5 Aug 2025): interactive 720p/24fps worlds for minutes ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/08/05/deepmind-thinks-genie-3-world-model-presents-stepping-stone-towards-agi/)). Trained for prediction/generation, not judgment. Use for monitoring so far = anomaly scores: world-model + conformal thresholds classify success/failure/OOD at >90% on Spot gauge inspection ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.16182)); V-JEPA-2 latent innovation as satellite anomaly score ([SSRN](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=6521219)). NVIDIA's own warehouse-safety blueprint has a conventional CV pipeline generate incidents and Cosmos Reason2-8B only analyze/report them post hoc ([NVIDIA docs](https://docs.nvidia.com/vss/3.0.0/warehouse-docs/3.0.0/agents.html)). No one issues compliance verdicts from a world model (none found).

## 4. Direct "learned compliance" and rule-grounded post-training

- **VLM-as-inspector results**: 1,100 construction images, GPT-4o BERTScore 0.906 / Gemini-1.5-Pro 0.888 — a text-similarity score, not violation accuracy; latency flagged ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.09083)). ConstructionSite-10K (Chen & Zou, Data-Centric Engineering, accepted Feb 2026): zero/few-shot generalization "notable" but "additional training is needed" before real sites; worst on rules needing spatial relations (harness at height, excavator radius) ([Cambridge](https://www.cambridge.org/core/services/aop-cambridge-core/content/view/4F9F8B39B34FD6F2B201C9947CDF42E8); detail from secondary summary, **partly unverified**). Prompt-ensembled Molmo-7B on it: P 58.2 / R 79.6 / F1 67.2 (reported via [arXiv 2511.15720](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.15720); attribution **unverified**). HomeSafeBench (Sep 2025): best closed-source model 34.7 F1 vs human 98.0; models systematically under-report hazards ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.23690)). A 2026 indoor-construction study ranks "hallucination suppression" as the most safety-critical metric ([ScienceDirect](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S266616592600205X), paywalled, **unverified**). Failure modes across these: missed hazards, hallucinated objects, no numbers (SpatialVLM: GPT-4V 1% valid numbers), and no reproducible evidence.
- **Production**: Intenseye / Protex AI / Voxel are detectors + human-configured zones and rules (Protex: "configurable rule builder… by zone, count and direction", [Protex](https://www.protex.ai/area-control)). MonitorVLM-v2 (Aug 2026) is the only deployed VLM verdict system found: 4 months, 10 feeds, underground mine; it deliberately *restricts* the VLM to predicting a rule-ID over a finite regulatory decision space, with entropy-based triage to humans; 2.78x more confirmed violations than manual review, no measurement claims ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.00975)).
- **Compile rules, then execute**: GuardEn (EMNLP 2026) compiles safety policies into atomic propositions as executable code and grounds them in scene graphs; +9.8 F1 over the strongest end-to-end baseline on SafetyVisionBench ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.18328)). CompAgent (Nov 2025) is an agentic VLM + tools for GDPR/pharma/broadcast rules ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.00171)). Logic-LM: LLM→formal program→solver, +39.2% over standard prompting and +18.4% over CoT on five logic datasets ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.12295)). nl2spec (CAV 2023) NL→LTL with interactive sub-translations ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.04864)); LLASP (KR 2024) fine-tuned small model for ASP, large semantic-correctness gains ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.18723)); GPT-3.5/4 few-shot building code → LegalRuleML (Jul 2024, no accuracy reported) ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.21060)); LLM→SMT constraints for financial statutes, 86.2% correct on 87 cases (Jan 2026) ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.06181)); ARc autoformalization with redundant translations + SMT equivalence checks, >99% soundness, now a commercial guardrail (Nov 2025) ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.09008)).
- **Verifier-in-the-loop RL on rules**: P4IR (Jun 2026) SFT+GRPO to emit building-code rule IR, +23.8% tree-edit-distance over SFT, fewer false positives, beats Claude Opus/GPT-5.2 zero-shot ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.22402)); Catala-verified law as RL reward (Jun 2026) ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.23913)); Legal-Δ RLVR (Aug 2025) ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.12281)). RLVR on *spatial* outputs exists (Sec. 2). RLVR that rewards a *regulatory verdict on a 3D scene*: none found.

## 5. Spec / knowledge graphs

ACCORD (Horizon Europe) built AEC3PO, the first AEC compliance-checking ontology, with RDF checks in GraphDB ([CORDIS](https://cordis.europa.eu/project/id/101056973), [Zenodo](https://zenodo.org/records/15848519)); VTT formalises building codes as KGs ([VTT](https://cris.vtt.fi/en/publications/formalization-of-building-codes-and-regulations-in-knowledge-grap/)); LegalRuleML/LegalDocML adapted to AEC ([ITC](https://itc.scix.net/paper/lc3-2017-012)). Entity alignment of model classes to regulated classes: Zhang & El-Gohary 2023 transformer relation-classifier aligning IFC concepts to code concepts, "good performance", numbers not in abstract (**unverified**) ([Illinois](https://experts.illinois.edu/en/publications/transformer-based-approach-for-automated-context-aware-ifc-regula/)); SGR-BIM (Jun 2026) cross-modal KG of intent, regulation semantics, and BIM geometry: 84.3% on 679 expert-verified fire-code queries ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.12065)); KG-augmented policy QA helps all five LLMs tested (Apr 2026) ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.27713)). For *perception-derived* scene graphs linked to a requirement graph with reported precision: nothing found — GuardEn and RieMind are the closest. For your rules, ISO 10218-2:2025 (speed-and-separation, zones) is the target spec ([ISO](https://www.iso.org/standard/73934.html)); temporal-logic formalisation of standards has precedent (ISO 34502 → STL with RSS distance, [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.18764)).

## Synthesis

**(a) What training buys, where it stops.** Pre/post-training reliably delivers open-vocabulary detection, grounding and pointing (Molmo, ER 1.5/1.6), counting (~90%), and *qualitative* relations (SpatialRGPT 92%). It does not deliver metric reliability: the field's own success criteria are "within ±25%" (SpatialRGPT, ~35-59% on distances) or "within 2x" (SpatialVLM, 37%), and Gemini-1.5 Pro sits near chance on absolute distance in VSI-Bench. It does not deliver auditability: three independent 2025-26 critiques show benchmark scores driven by language/2D shortcuts (ACL 2025, ICLR 2026, NoSense). It does not transfer to your distribution: every dataset above is indoor homes/offices (ScanNet, ARKitScenes, ADT) or construction photos; ConstructionSite-10K concludes additional training is needed. And it is data-hungry: 120k-27M samples per model; you have 2-10 workcells.

**(b) The architecture mature teams converge on.** Foundation models produce *symbols with uncertainty* (instances, poses, dimensions, confidences); a symbolic layer judges; the explanation is the evidence chain. Evidence: robotics stacks keep an explicit scene graph (Hydra, ConceptGraphs [ICRA 2024](https://arxiv.org/abs/2309.16650), Khronos); RieMind beats end-to-end VLMs by giving the LLM geometric tools over a 3DSG; GuardEn beats end-to-end safety VLMs by compiling rules to code executed over scene graphs; Logic-LM and ARc get faithfulness from a solver; MonitorVLM-v2 — the one deployed VLM verdict system — collapses the output to a finite rule-ID space with uncertainty triage; NVIDIA's warehouse blueprint and Protex/Intenseye keep detection and rules separate and let the VLM narrate. Your current design is this architecture.

**(c) What a 2-10-workcell team can post-train — and must not.** Post-train only the symbol producers, with objectives that have ground truth you can measure:
1. *Detector/segmenter*: LoRA or few-shot adapt an open-vocab model to your regulated classes (guard, light curtain, e-stop, robot base, fence) on multi-view crops from your cells plus synthetic renders; SpatialVLM/SpatialRGPT prove depth+detector-derived synthetic supervision works.
2. *Metric scale calibration*: not a model, a procedure — fit reconstruction scale to known-size references per cell and report residual error; this is what moves you from ±25% to cm.
3. *Predicate layer*: compute geometric predicates (distance, clearance, support, inside-zone) from the reconstruction; if you learn anything here, learn a small classifier on geometric features with 3DSSG-style per-predicate recall reported, never pixels→predicate.
4. *Rule compiler*: prompt/fine-tune an LLM to emit executable checks from ISO 10218-2 / ISO/TS 15066 clauses (P4IR, LLASP, ARc pattern), validated by unit tests on held-out clauses; keep clause IDs in the output so every verdict cites its rule.
5. *Class linker*: let a VLM *propose* regulated-class membership with confidence; a human confirms until your measured precision justifies auto-accept.
Do not train photo→verdict: 34.7 F1 is the state of the art on a far easier home-hazard task, numeric outputs are shortcut-prone, and you cannot produce an evidence chain or a defensible OOD story from it.

**(d) The time axis.** Pre/post-train the *dynamic symbol producers*: tracking, pose/keypoints, change detection (DovSG/ST-OVSG/Khronos-style long-term vs short-term), and optionally a world-model anomaly score as a prior (gauge-inspection result >90%). Keep judgment symbolic: express rules as temporal logic (STL/LTL) over per-frame scene graphs — "whenever robot speed > v, separation ≥ S_p" — as done for ISO 34502; learned video predicates (R@20 ≈ 40% on Action Genome, 4% on PVSG) are not verdict-grade. 4D scene graphs (Khronos, Aion) are the right container for long-term state, with flow/occupancy models as learned attributes on nodes.

**(e) Recommendations.**
1. Keep the explicit scene graph + rule engine; make every verdict carry {instance ids, measured values ± uncertainty, rule clause id} as the audit artifact.
2. Spend your labels on detector adaptation and per-cell scale calibration; publish a per-predicate and per-measurement error table on your own cells before any claim.
3. Compile rules from the standard text with an LLM into executable checks, unit-tested per clause; treat the LLM as a compiler, not a judge.
4. Use VLMs (Gemini-ER 1.5/1.6, Molmo-class, Qwen3-VL) for pointing, counting, and candidate proposal with confidence; gate low-confidence symbols to a human, MonitorVLM-v2-style.
5. For video, adopt STL monitoring over per-frame graphs now; evaluate world models only as anomaly priors; revisit end-to-end approaches when a benchmark without shortcuts (Real-3DQA, ReVSI) shows cm-level metric reliability on OOD industrial scenes.
