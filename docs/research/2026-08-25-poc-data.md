# POC data acquisition — photos / video / policy+VLM

Date 2026-08-25. Data-acquisition pass for the three upcoming POCs.
Everything below is on disk now; the owner-facing overview is
`outputs/datasets/poc_atlas.png` (12 labeled thumbnails: 8 photo
candidates + first/action frames of both acquired video clips).
Licenses were re-verified against the source before every download; the
one candidate whose GT turned out withheld is documented and skipped.

## 1. Acquired: MEVA video + annotations (CC-BY-4.0) — 439 MB

License re-verified at download time: mevadata.org states "All MEVA
data is available for use under a CC BY-4.0 license"; the license text
is archived at `outputs/datasets/meva/MEVA-data-license.txt`. Videos
pulled from the open S3 bucket (`mevadata-public-01`, no signing);
annotations and KRTD camera models from
https://gitlab.kitware.com/meva/meva-data-repo (raw master).

Two 5-minute 1920x1080 30 fps clips, chosen from the intersection of
Kitware's `list-of-annotated-meva-clips.txt` with camera metadata
(`meva-camera-daily-status.txt` int/ext flags), decode-verified with
OpenCV (9001/9009 frames read):

| Clip | Size | Why this one |
|---|---|---|
| `2018-03-05.13-15-00.13-20-00.bus.G506.r13.avi` | 137 MB | **Exterior** fixed cam on building entrance + parking: vehicle stops/reverses/U-turns, person exits vehicle, opens trunk, `person_unloads_vehicle` (frames 2860–2991), carries heavy object, transfers object — loading-yard analog. **Exact-window KRTD camera model exists** (one of only ~29 clips with one), so the floor-plane ray-cast lift runs with a real calibrated camera. |
| `2018-03-15.15-00-00.15-05-00.school.G299.r13.avi` | 146 MB | **Interior** fixed cam (gym): people crossing a large open floor, pick-up/put-down object, enter/exit through structure — indoor workcell-floor analog for zone entry/exit and person-in-zone. Same-date KRTD for G299 also fetched (08:34 window; fixed mount). |

On disk under `outputs/datasets/meva/`:

- `videos/` — the two `.avi` (283 MB)
- `annotations/` — per-clip Kitware `activities.yml` (activity types +
  actor IDs + frame spans), `geom.yml` (per-frame per-actor bounding
  boxes — the track GT), `types.yml` (actor classes) for both clips
- `krtd/` — `bus.G506` (exact window) and `school.G299` KRTD camera
  models
- `mevid/` — `mevid-v1-annotation-data.zip` + extracted tracklet
  name/info lists (12 MB zip; global person-ID tracklet index, 158 IDs /
  8,092 tracklets). The multi-GB MEVID bbox image crops
  (`mevid-v1-bbox-*.tgz`, 14+32 GB) were NOT pulled — the tracklet
  lists are enough to know which cameras/IDs overlap our clips; crops
  only matter for re-ID training, which we don't do.
- `preview_frames/` — decode-check frames used in the atlas

Total: 439 MB, well under the 1.5 GB bound.

## 4. Recommendation

For the **video POC**, lead with
`2018-03-05.13-15-00.13-20-00.bus.G506.r13.avi`: it is the only real
clip we hold with the full trifecta — activity GT (`activities.yml` +
per-frame `geom.yml` boxes), an exact-window KRTD camera model for the
deterministic floor-plane lift, and an EHS-shaped event sequence
(vehicle stops → person exits → trunk → unload → heavy carry) inside
frames ~2500–3800, so a 45-second slice at 1 fps (~45 frames) exercises
zone entry/exit, person–vehicle min-distance, and track identity for
under a dollar of fal calls; keep `school.G299` as the interior
cross-check that the same pipeline holds indoors. Synthetic Warehouse_016
(already on disk, world GT) remains the scale/accuracy yardstick via
`scripts/mtmc_eval.py`. For the **photos POC**, run the owner factory
photo first (it is the domain we sell), then LOCO #2/#4/#5 (fence+gate
zone, person–vehicle separation, aisle obstruction) as the breadth set,
and MTMC #7/#8 when a GT-scored number is wanted. The **policy+VLM POC**
should reuse the same images — the owner photo plus LOCO #2 make the
cleanest policy-compilation demo pair (one guarded-machine policy, one
restricted-zone/gate policy), keeping all three POCs on one shared,
already-licensed data pool.
