# Starter policy set — illustrative, not certified

# Paraphrased workplace-safety requirements used to exercise the policy
# compiler. These are NOT quotations of any standard and carry no
# compliance authority; they exist so the compiler and the deterministic
# evaluator have realistic prose to chew on. Real deployment must compile
# from the operator's own reviewed rule text.

- Movable equipment must be kept at least 0.6 m clear of any machine guarding fence.
- No person may stand within 1.2 m of an industrial robot arm while it is enclosed by guarding.
- Marked walkways must remain clear: no pallet may come within 0.5 m of a floor marking line.
- A fire extinguisher must be within 15 m of every control panel on the shop floor.
- Stacked pallets must not exceed 2.5 m in height.
- Portable ladders showing more than 10 degrees of lean must be taken out of service.
- Storage racks must not encroach into a marked walkway at all.
- Every worker on the production floor must wear a hard hat at all times.
- Emergency stop buttons must be repainted red every two years.
