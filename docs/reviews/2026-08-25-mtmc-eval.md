Date 2026-08-25. Runner `scripts/mtmc_eval.py`. Dataset:
`nvidia/PhysicalAI-SmartSpaces`, `MTMC_Tracking_2025/val/Warehouse_016`.

## Slice downloaded (bounded)

One scene, 666 MB into `outputs/datasets/mtmc/`: `Warehouse_016` (val) —
`calibration.json`, `ground_truth.json` (9 000 frames, world coordinates
in metres), `map.png`, and 4 of 12 camera videos (`Camera`, `Camera_01`,
`Camera_03`, `Camera_07` — the views with the most simultaneous objects).
Frames extracted at indices 0 / 3600 / 7200 → 12 mono images (elevated
CCTV-style viewpoints). Video/GT sync was eyeball-verified by drawing the
GT visible boxes on an extracted frame (they wrap objects tightly) and is
checked automatically every run: projecting GT centres through the
provided `cameraMatrix` lands a median 7.7 px from the visible-box
centres (n=280) — calibration, GT, and frame extraction are aligned.

## Protocol

Questions are object pairs visible in the same camera frame with boxes
>= 2000 px^2 and GT centroid distance >= 0.5 m (cap 12/image): 140
questions over 12 images (Person x118, FourierGR1T2 humanoid x112,
Transporter AMR x50 pair-slots; GT distances 1.4–14.6 m, median 5.8 m).
ZERO SAM spend — the dataset's own visible boxes are the regions (boxes,
not masks, so background rides along; a range-MAD trim keeps the dominant
surface, same recipe as `nvwarehouse_eval`).

Both distance semantics are scored against their MATCHING GT (the
boundary map showed protocol mismatch alone moves scores): predicted
centroid distance vs GT `3d location` distance, and predicted min point
distance vs the min distance between GT oriented 3D boxes (surface-
sampled; quantisation <= ~9 cm for person-sized boxes).

## Results (n=140, all answered, no image failures)

| Configuration | median rel err | <=10% | <=25% |
|---|---|---|---|
| Unanchored mono, centroid | 60.5% | 2.1% | 5.7% |
| Unanchored mono, min-dist | 66.8% | 1.4% | 3.6% |
| **MoGe-2 auto-anchored, centroid** | **17.4%** | 30.0% | 61.4% |
| **MoGe-2 auto-anchored, min-dist** | 25.3% | 22.1% | 50.0% |

MoGe per-image scale factors: median 2.66 (range 2.38–3.71 over 12
images) — the same ~2.5x constant-gauge error the boundary map measured
on the NVIDIA warehouse benchmark (2.50), reproduced on a second
synthetic warehouse the models never saw.

Per-camera, MoGe-anchored centroid median: `Camera` 10.4%, `Camera_01`
10.0%, `Camera_03` 18.3%, **`Camera_07` 107.9%**. Excluding Camera_07
(106 questions): centroid 12.9% median / 78.3% <=25%, min-dist 17.7% /
61.3%.

## Reading

1. **The scale-anchor thesis transfers out-of-domain.** On a scene,
   object set (humanoid robots, AMRs), and viewpoint style (high-mounted
   CCTV) none of our prior evals used, unanchored mono repeats its
   known failure (60% median, the constant gauge) and the MoGe-2 auto
   anchor recovers it to 10–18% median on 3 of 4 cameras — in line with
   the 9.0% the anchor scored in-domain on nvwarehouse.
2. **Camera_07 is a real failure, reported as measured.** Its MoGe scales
   (3.44–3.71) disagree with every other view (2.38–2.71), and anchored
   predictions come out 2–5x too large: on that viewpoint MapAnything's
   relative geometry itself is distorted, and the anchor multiplies the
   distortion. No parameter was tuned to hide it; it is the largest
   residual and the clearest argument for the production stance of
   multi-view capture plus per-run scale confidence.
3. **Protocol matters, again.** Centroid-vs-centroid beats min-vs-min
   (17.4% vs 25.3%) even with each scored against its matching GT: the
   min protocol inherits binding noise from box regions (background
   pixels survive the trim at object silhouettes). The clearance rule
   wants min-distance, so the min-vs-min number is the honest one for
   product claims on this kind of region evidence.

## Residual error, stated plainly

With the auto anchor and the centroid protocol, half the answers are
still off by more than 17%; a quarter of camera-views (1 of 4) produced
geometry the anchor could not fix, taking that view's median above 100%.
Nothing here reaches the multiview real-photo tier (14–18 cm MAE); these
are mono, far-field (median 5.8 m), region-not-mask measurements and they
look like it.

## Reproduce

```
uv run --env-file .env --with imageio-ffmpeg python scripts/mtmc_eval.py          # free preview
uv run --env-file .env --with imageio-ffmpeg python scripts/mtmc_eval.py --live   # spend once
```

Geometry and MoGe scalars cache under `outputs/mtmc_v1/`; re-scores are
free. Frame extraction uses the imageio-ffmpeg bundled binary (this
machine has no system ffmpeg).
