# Robot-arm POC — arm states + sweep envelopes on DROID, FK-validated

Date 2026-08-25. Motion POC v2 on factory robot-arm imagery: a real Franka
FR3 in the curtained AUTOLab cell (DROID raw episode, CC-BY-4.0), judged by
the commodity stack (SAM 3.1 masks + MoGe-2 metric mono depth) and validated
against exact kinematic ground truth (Panda forward kinematics from the
episode's own joint record). Data verdicts and the FK recipe:
`docs/research/2026-08-25-robotcell-data.md`. Prior stack patterns:
`docs/demos/2026-08-25-poc-video.md`.

## Ground truth layer (deterministic, $0)

- **FK**: standard Franka Panda modified-DH table (7 joints + 0.107 m flange
  offset) reproduces the h5's own `cartesian_position` record with
  **0.000 mm max error over all 182 timesteps** — the recorded pose IS the
  FK of the joint states, so link-frame origins are exact arm geometry.
- **Conventions proven, not assumed**: recorded euler angles are extrinsic
  x-y-z; camera extrinsics are the camera pose in the robot base frame. The
  proof: only under 'xyz' does the wrist camera's per-frame extrinsic stay a
  constant rigid transform from the FK flange (0.0 deg drift over the
  episode, position offset a constant 88.0 mm).
- **GT arm state**: ACTIVE when the joint-velocity norm from the h5 exceeds
  0.05 rad/s, else PARKED. The episode has three PARKED stretches (steps
  0-8, 48-54, 171-181) and fast sweeps in between (max ‖q̇‖ 1.45 rad/s).
- **TRUE sweep envelope**: plan-view convex hull of the FK kinematic
  skeleton (link segments sampled every 5 cm) over all 182 steps.
- **Zone**: TRUE envelope buffered +0.30 m (the keep-clear analog).
  A second, deliberately violated **keep-out line** sits 0.15 m inside the
  episode's max-x reach, so ground truth contains genuine FAIL timestamps
  and silent misses are measurable.

## Estimated layer (commodity stack)

- 90 frames sampled from the ext1 exterior camera (every 2nd control step,
  ~7 Hz over the ~12.7 s episode; the MP4 carries one frame per control
  step).
- SAM 3.1 `image-rle` masks, prompts "robot arm" -> "robotic arm" ordered
  fallback, house cache + `--live` gate + 120-call cap.
- MoGe-2 (replicate) metric point map per frame -> nearest-hit depth map
  (cached npz) -> mask pixels (eroded 2 px, stride 3, range < 2 m) lifted to
  a camera-frame cloud -> robot base frame via the h5 extrinsics.
- Estimated state: mask XOR/union ratio between consecutive samples > 0.05
  = ACTIVE. Estimated envelope: plan-view hull of all lifted arm points.

All thresholds above are stated once and never tuned; disagreements are
reported, not fixed.

## Numbers (from `outputs/arm_poc_v1/report.json`)

**R-A arm state** (est mask-motion vs FK joint-velocity truth, 89 scored
samples): **agreement 0.899**, 9 disagreements, all reported. 7 are
threshold-straddlers around state transitions (mask change 0.03-0.05
against ‖q̇‖ 0.04-0.10 — both sides hovering at their own thresholds).
2 are real detector misses: steps 130 and 138 have fast joint motion
(‖q̇‖ 0.22 and 0.65 rad/s) but mask change just under 0.05 — motion mostly
along the camera axis, which barely changes the silhouette. A
silhouette-based state detector is blind to approach-axis motion; a second
camera or the 3D clouds fix this.

**R-B sweep envelope** (plan view, robot base frame):

| | IoU | est area | under-est max/mean | over-est max/mean |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| raw MoGe metric | **0.337** | 0.357 m² | **0.385 / 0.090 m** | 0.363 / 0.122 m |
| FK-scale-calibrated (×0.921) | 0.396 | 0.303 m² | 0.383 / 0.087 m | 0.309 / 0.091 m |

TRUE envelope area 0.254 m². The under-estimate — the number a safety
engineer needs — says a fence drawn from this camera alone would be up to
**0.385 m too small**, and that error is a *visibility* error, not a depth
error: the base-side half of the sweep happens behind the curtain and off
the right edge of the frame (the plan-view figure shows the estimated hull
covering only the distal/visible half of the TRUE triangle). The
over-estimate (up to 0.36 m outward) is depth noise + the ~5-10 deg
exterior-extrinsics rotation error + arm surface vs kinematic skeleton.

**MoGe-2 metric lift verdict: good, not perfect.** Estimated FOV ~100 deg
(ZED 2 is ~102 deg). FK-anchored range-scale fit: **0.921** (MoGe ranges
~8% long), median point-to-FK-skeleton residual **10.8 cm raw -> 10.0 cm
scaled** (the kinematic skeleton sits ~5-6 cm inside the arm surface, so
the geometric floor is ~6 cm). The single-scalar calibration buys +0.06
IoU — measurable but not transformative. Contrary to the Q-Spatial
close-range worry, metric mono depth was usable out of the box on this
tabletop cell; visibility and stale extrinsics dominate the error budget.

**R-C banded zone rules** (est banded ±0.35 m, GT exact; zero-silent-miss
is the target property):

- *Keep-clear zone = TRUE envelope + 0.30 m* (GT: PASS on all 90 samples).
  Estimate: 88 NEEDS_REVIEW, 2 PASS, 0 FAIL, **0 silent misses**. Median
  est arm extremity sits 0.25 m inside the zone boundary — inside the
  ±0.35 band, so the verdict is NEEDS_REVIEW by construction. The honest
  reading: **a 0.30 m margin is unverifiable at a ±0.35 m tier**; margins
  must exceed the error band (or the tier must tighten) before PASS can
  ever be certified. Max est excursion beyond the zone: +0.06 m (never a
  false FAIL).
- *Keep-out line* x = 0.344 m, 0.15 m inside the TRUE max reach (GT: FAIL
  on 65/90 samples — genuine violations). Estimate: 4 FAIL + 61
  NEEDS_REVIEW on the 65 true violations, 25 NEEDS_REVIEW on the 25 true
  PASSes, **0 silent misses** — the estimator never certified a violation
  frame as clean. Raw agreement is 0.044 only because NEEDS_REVIEW ≠
  FAIL/PASS in the strict count; every violation was flagged for review or
  failed outright.

**FANUC 224 px cross-check** (`fanuc_report.json`, Berkeley FANUC ep. 0,
state axis only — the dataset ships no camera calibration): SAM masked the
arm on **34/34** sampled frames (4.3k-11.4k px masks) — no resolution
floor hit at 224 px. State agreement **0.758** (33 scored): 7 of 8 misses
are est-ACTIVE/gt-PARKED at the 0.8 s sampling stride, where the interval
spans motion around a parked endpoint — a temporal-aliasing artifact of
sparse sampling, not a mask failure.

## Data-quality findings (worth knowing before the next arm capture)

- **The h5's own record is self-consistent to 0.00 mm** (FK vs recorded
  cartesian) — joint-state GT is as good as GT gets.
- **The exterior camera extrinsics are only approximately right.** The
  wrist camera's extrinsics are FK-derived (exact); the fixed cameras'
  rotation is off by roughly 5-10 deg (projected FK skeleton lands ~80-150
  px from the arm at 0.3-0.6 m range). DROID cameras get bumped and
  re-calibrated occasionally; we used the values as recorded, and this
  error is inside the R-B over-estimate. A workcell deployment needs a
  calibration check ritual, not a one-time file.
- **No DROID exterior view sees the whole arm.** All 5 labs' exterior
  cameras are manipulation close-ups; some of the arm is always out of
  frame or behind the curtain. Camera placement — "the camera must see the
  entire fence-relevant volume" — is a deployment requirement this POC can
  now quantify (it is the 0.385 m under-estimate).

## Honest limitations

- **A teleop lab cell is not a factory cell.** Real fenced cells have
  longer sightlines (better for mono depth), painted floors and fences
  (better segmentation anchors), but also motion blur, sparks, and
  occluding stock. The machinery proven here — FK truth, cached
  SAM+MoGe lift, banded deterministic verdicts — transfers; the numbers do
  not.
- **The mask-motion state detector is silhouette-bound** (misses
  approach-axis motion, steps 130/138). The lifted 3D clouds are already
  computed and would resolve this; not done here to keep the estimator
  honest to its stated definition.
- **Envelope truth is the kinematic skeleton hull**, not the swept volume
  of the arm's actual surface — the TRUE envelope is itself ~6 cm inside
  the metal. The est-vs-true discrepancy numbers inherit that offset.
- **One episode, one camera, 90 samples, 12.7 s.** No benchmark claims.
- **Person-arm distance is explicitly deferred**: DROID scenes are
  unstaffed, and no permissively-licensed human+robot dataset with both
  poses exists in our scan. CHICO (KUKA arm, 20 operators, 226 real
  collisions, both-skeleton GT) is the unlock and needs one email to the
  Verona authors for license terms
  (docs/research/2026-08-25-robotcell-data.md).
