# Video POC — state/trajectory verdicts on real fixed-camera video

Date 2026-08-25. First complete video proof-of-concept: a real MEVA
surveillance clip in, deterministic per-timestamp PASS/FAIL/NEEDS_REVIEW
state and trajectory verdicts out, every number auditable against the
clip's Kitware ground truth. Stack per
`docs/research/2026-08-25-motion-trajectory-scan.md` (pilot stack a),
data per `docs/research/2026-08-25-poc-data.md`.

## What ran

Clip `2018-03-05.13-15-00.13-20-00.bus.G506.r13.avi` (exterior fixed
camera, 1080p 30 fps, CC-BY-4.0), segment frames 2500-3800 (~43 s): a
vehicle stops, a person exits, opens the trunk, unloads, carries a heavy
object, two people talk beside the car, then leave. The clip is one of
~29 MEVA clips with an exact-window KRTD camera model, so the 3D lift
uses a real calibration, not a reconstruction.

Pipeline (`scripts/video_poc.py`, all stages disk-cached):

1. **Frames** — 44 frames at 1 fps (2500..3790 step 30), full-res JPEG
   via OpenCV sequential decode.
2. **Masks** — fal SAM 3.1 `image-rle`, prompts `person` and
   `car`->`vehicle` (ordered fallback; `car` always hit, the fallback
   never fired). House pattern: disk cache, `--live` gate printing the
   planned call count, 150-call budget cap, exponential backoff,
   per-frame skip on failure. Zero Gemini, zero MapAnything.
3. **Identity** — ByteTrack from the Apache-2.0 `roboflow/trackers`
   package (`uv run --with trackers`, import name `trackers`) over mask
   bboxes, one tracker per class, timestamps in seconds. The package
   worked; the IoU-greedy fallback was not needed. One trap worth
   recording: the library measures `lost_track_buffer` in 30 fps frame
   units regardless of `frame_rate` (budget = buffer/30 seconds), so
   1 fps sampling needs `lost_track_buffer=150` for a 5 s keepalive —
   with the naive value every track expired between samples and all ids
   came back -1.
4. **3D lift** — ground-plane ray-cast through the KRTD model:
   undistort the bbox bottom-center pixel (radial k1/k2, fixed-point
   inversion), cast from the camera center to z=0, world metres.
   Deterministic, $0. Camera sits 6.8 m up, ~30 m from the action.
   Vehicles additionally lift both bottom corners into a ground segment
   (near-side edge) for distance measurements.
5. **Judgment** — shapely + the mono tier band (±0.35 m,
   `ERROR_BUDGET_MONO_M`) as the spatial buffer; a verdict inside the
   band says NEEDS_REVIEW instead of flipping on noise.
   - **R1 keep-clear zone**: per-timestamp person-in-zone state. The
     zone polygon is the rectangle over the lifted GT positions of the
     trunk/unload actors, buffered 0.75 m — i.e. the apron where the
     unloading actually happened (the loading-dock analog). In
     production the site owner draws this on the SceneMap; deriving it
     from GT here is documented, deterministic authorship, not a fit.
   - **R2 person-to-vehicle min distance** vs a 2.0 m separation
     threshold (forklift keep-apart analog), person point to vehicle
     near-edge segment.
   - **R3 person speed** vs 1.5 m/s walking pace; the band propagates
     to speed as 2×0.35 m / 1 s = 0.7 m/s.
   Every rule runs twice: on estimated tracks and on the GT geom boxes
   lifted through the *same* ray-cast. Bags are excluded (no prompt for
   them); MEVA geom ids are per-activity actor ids (the same physical
   car appears under six ids), handled by comparing scene-level states
   per timestamp rather than pairing track ids.

## Commands

From the repo root (data paths default to `outputs/datasets/meva/`):

```bash
# dry run: extracts frames, prints planned SAM calls and cost, exits 2
uv run --with trackers python scripts/video_poc.py

# live (paid) run — 88 calls, $0.88, ~10 min
uv run --env-file .env --with trackers python scripts/video_poc.py --live

# every rerun after that is free (~2 min, cache hits)
uv run --with trackers python scripts/video_poc.py
```

Offline tests: `uv run python -m pytest -q tests/test_video_poc.py`
(KRTD parse + project/lift round-trip on a synthetic camera, banded
verdicts, GT parsing, judge timelines, ATE gating, the spend gate).

## Cost

88 SAM calls (44 frames × 2 prompts), **$0.88** total, 0 failures, well
under the ≤150-call budget. Tracking, lift, judgment, rendering: $0.

## Numbers (from `outputs/video_poc_v1/report.json`)

Position accuracy of the estimated tracks against GT boxes lifted
through the same camera (nearest-GT match, 3 m gate):

| Class | median | p90 | matched points |
|---|---|---|---|
| person | **0.45 m** | 0.77 m | 37 |
| vehicle | **1.02 m** | 1.08 m | 17 |

The vehicle offset is systematic: the SAM mask bottom sits ~10 px above
the GT box bottom (annotators include shadow/ground contact), and at
30 m range 1 px ≈ 0.1 m. 191 estimated vehicle points fell beyond the
gate — those are the four parked cars across the street, which are real
but never annotated by MEVA (it only boxes activity actors); 16 GT
person points had no estimate within 3 m (detection misses, below).

Per-rule verdict agreement, estimated vs GT-derived timelines over the
44 timestamps (the headline honesty number):

| Rule | agreement | both-observed | disagreements |
|---|---|---|---|
| R1 zone state | 0.68 | 0.68 | 14 |
| R2 min distance | 0.41 | 0.50 | 26 |
| R3 speed | 0.64 | 0.65 | 16 |

("both-observed" excludes timestamps where either side has NO_DATA.)

## Where the disagreements come from (no tuning applied)

- **GT annotation gaps, not estimation errors** (the largest R2 cause,
  17 of 26): MEVA only boxes actors while an activity annotation runs.
  The SUV sits parked in plain view from 3220-3610 while the estimate
  correctly measures a person 1.2-1.6 m from it (FAIL); GT has no
  vehicle box there at all (NO_DATA). Same for persons walking between
  activities (est sees them in the zone at 3010, GT is empty → GT PASS).
  Each disagreement in the report carries the GT box count at that
  frame (`gt_boxes`) so these are separable.
- **Band cases** (most R1 disagreements): the talkers hover within
  ±0.35 m of the zone boundary; the estimate says NEEDS_REVIEW where GT
  says FAIL. That is the band doing its job — the two trajectories are
  a few tenths of a metre apart, which is exactly what the tier cannot
  resolve.
- **The edge-clipped person** (the main R3 cause): the second talker
  stands half out of frame at the right edge; their mask bottom jumps
  between feet and knee (y 176-204) as clothing/legs clip, the
  feet-on-ground lift turns that into 3-5 m position jumps, and the
  speed rule reads 4-5 m/s phantom sprints (FAIL at 3220-3400 vs GT
  PASS). Visible as the long green excursions in the top-down plot.
  This is the documented failure mode of the monocular lift.
- **Detection misses**: SAM returned no person at 3040 (0 masks) and
  the person at the car door at 2530-2620 (occluded by the door);
  frame 3730 lost the SUV mask, so R2 silently measured 74 m to the
  nearest *detected* vehicle across the street — an honest reminder
  that "min distance over detected objects" degrades to PASS when the
  near object blinks out.
- **Track fragmentation**: 6 estimated person tracks for ~3 physical
  people (misses reset ByteTrack's Kalman; new id after each gap).
  Scene-level states hide most of this, identity-based rules would not.

## Honest limitations

- **MEVA is real fixed-camera video but not a workcell.** People and a
  parked SUV at a building entrance stand in for workers and machinery.
  The machinery this POC proves — calibrated lift, identity over time,
  banded state timelines, GT cross-checks — transfers; the moving-fence
  and robot-arm states still need owner capture with measured ground
  truth (tape measure + stopwatch), as recorded in the motion scan.
- **Feet-on-ground is the load-bearing assumption.** It held to 0.45 m
  for fully-visible people and broke visibly (3-5 m) the moment feet
  left the frame. A workcell deployment scopes the camera so the zone
  floor is fully in view and gates out masks touching the image border.
- **GT here validates positions and states, not completeness**: MEVA
  cannot certify "zone empty" (it annotates activity actors only), so
  agreement on empty frames understates the estimate.
- **One camera, one clip, 44 timestamps.** The interior cross-check
  (school.G299) was not run; the numbers above are one scene, not a
  benchmark.
- 1 fps sampling puts the speed band at 0.7 m/s — walking-pace rules
  sit mostly in NEEDS_REVIEW territory by construction. Denser sampling
  (more SAM calls or self-hosted video mode) shrinks that band
  linearly.
