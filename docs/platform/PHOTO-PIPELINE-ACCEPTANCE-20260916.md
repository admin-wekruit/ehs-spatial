## Shared review and failure behavior

`review_models` reuses the same mesh/CAD/source validator as new generation. The
existing models were checked with fixed job inputs and zero new provider calls.
090 retained all original models and transforms: 16 rows are geometrically
consistent, eight are inconsistent, and all 24 lack an independent configured
shape review. 030 retained both button models and their exact quality bindings.
Reports show this as the last check, never as current physical compliance.

The viewer exposes observed CAD and current-model CAD as separate explicit
sources. Both use real mesh-triangle projection; selection remains the same
entity when switching. Public 090 and local 030 reverse CAD selection were
verified in the browser against photo, model preview, details and URL identity.
030's successful review also advanced the editable report URL to the new revision;
reload preserved the revision and selection. Published revisions remain fixed.

All source surface creation now rejects zero-area triangles after the original
edge-discontinuity filter and drops unused vertices. It does not connect sparse
origin placeholders into invented surfaces. Unsupported observations and photos
produce explicit errors while successful neighboring observations/photos remain.
Normal fresh-capture analysis and rebuild share that same context-error result;
a collapsed photo with empty discovery cannot report successful empty analysis.
Synthetic tests cover all-collapsed and mixed valid/invalid photos. They do not
substitute for the still-unfinished real fresh-photo release gate.

Segmentation preparation now derives the photo, box, observation revision and
optional reference mask from an owned scene observation. Verification reconstructs
these inputs from the immutable source; changing a payload and recomputing its
hash cannot substitute another source. Original JPEG bytes remain JPEG through
discovery, SAM and the geometry/depth image decoder. Declared MIME and decoded
format must agree; no source pixels are rewritten to fix a MIME label.

The installed fal SDK retries inference POSTs internally. The SAM adapter instead
makes one HTTP POST and durably records its returned request ID before polling.
Uncertain submission/result outcomes keep their reservation and never initiate
another inference merely by replaying the worker. Runtime evidence binds the
exact local adapter SHA and SDK version; the hosted endpoint does not expose an
immutable weights revision, and none is invented.

The actual full-image SAM response exposed a missing shared admission check.
Normal analysis, explicit resegmentation and research now reject wrong-grid or
non-boolean masks, empty masks, masks disjoint from the submitted box, and constant
full-image masks for a smaller object box. A full-image box remains allowed.
These are exact contradictions, not fitted IoU thresholds or clipped replacement
masks. Raw provider artifacts remain available; rejected resegmentation preserves
the previous observation mask, revision and derived surfaces. Research reports
an incomplete admission result separately from the completed provider call.

Empty text with a geometric prompt was an unverified hosted-service assumption.
The current shared SAM request uses the sole owning entity's saved label verbatim
alongside its box, with both label and owner frozen into the input. Missing or
ambiguous ownership and blank labels stop before reserving a call. This adds no
localization VLM call and no name or synonym guessing. It still requires real
segmentation quality validation before production release.

All segmentation callers now share an original-resolution context crop. The
integer crop extends three times the longest saved-box extent, clips to actual
photo bounds, and never resizes or pads the image. Both original and crop hashes,
the submitted local box and exact pixel-center translation are frozen. Admission
checks the provider's crop grid before mapping to original coordinates; zero
pixels outside a crop cannot disguise a full-crop mask as valid segmentation.
Crop-boundary contact is a diagnostic, not an invented acceptance threshold.
Valid empty provider results remain raw evidence and yield incomplete admission.

## Cross-photo identity: source and geometry failures remain distinct

Original signs and robot plates identify BOR1-030 in photos 1/2 and BOR1-090 in
photos 3/4 of the imported 030 project. This historical capture is not four
views of one workcell. Comparing all pairs does not make every pair an expected

a new canonical mask from the hash-verified, source-owned original mask when the

All 82 masks now load, including 48 and 412 valid support pixels for those two
buttons. Across 2,235 pairs, the replay still produces zero new merges: one
already-confirmed floor pair passes, and four overlapping floor/platform
candidates remain ambiguous. No production scene or identity changed in this
experiment. The before and after artifacts remain separately preserved.

Visual inspection of actual positive pairs explains why retaining those failures
is necessary. The same 030 robot has bilateral depth agreement of 18% and 43%;

pixels from its target mask, or outside the image in the reverse direction.
Source masks select the visible instances, but saved joint geometry does not
satisfy the unchanged cross-view checks. No generic coordinate-conversion bug
was demonstrated; self-reprojection alone cannot validate these correspondences.
The 030 cart is also visibly present in photo 2 but has no saved observation or
