# Video tab — production video-mono tier verification (2026-08-25)

The app's Video tab and `ehs_spatial/video.py` (uncalibrated video-mono tier)
were verified on two real MEVA clips through the production path
(`run_video_assessment`), after landing two first-live-contact fixes.

## Tier recap

Upload a fixed-camera clip → sampled frames (0.5/1/2 fps) → cached SAM masks →
ByteTrack association → MoGe-2 floor fit on 3 keyframes (plane + intrinsics)
→ ray-cast lift of track feet to the floor plane → banded temporal rules
(R1 keep-clear zone, R2 min separation, R3 max speed) → overlay GIF/strip,
top-down trajectories, `video_report.json`. No calibration input: positions
carry the ±0.35 m mono band; `capture_tier="video-mono"` in the manifest.
Calibrated reference: 0.45 m median person ATE with a real KRTD model
(docs/demos/2026-08-25-poc-video.md).

## Fixes found by first live contact (offline suite could not see them)

1. **MoGe clouds are not pixel grids.** The real `pointcloud_ply` drops
   invalid pixels (bus.G506: 2,006,624 pts vs 1920×1072 = 2,058,240), so
   `_organize_cloud` → grid-based pinhole fit failed on every keyframe.
   Fix: the live MoGe call now also caches `intrinsics_json` (MoGe's own
   normalized 3×3) beside the .ply; when the grid path fails, intrinsics are
   scaled to full-frame pixels and floor candidates are selected by
   projecting points into the lower 45 % of the image.
2. **MoGe .ply is in a GL frame** (y up, z back — all z < 0). `_moge_cloud`
   now auto-detects via median-z sign and flips to OpenCV convention,
   the same recipe as `scripts/arm_poc.py::cloud_to_depth`.

Both are covered by
`test_floor_fit_falls_back_to_moge_intrinsics_on_non_grid_cloud`.

## Run 1 — bus.G506 cached ($0 SAM + 3 MoGe calls)

`runs/video-bus-g506`, 44 frames @1 fps from frame 2500, labels person/car,
SAM cache seeded from `outputs/video_poc_v1` (same file format).

- Floor: fitted, camera height 3.444 m, keyframe spread **0.015 m**,
  inlier fraction 0.53.
- Tracks: 5 car + 6 person. R2 min separation 0.285 m < 2.0 m → **FAIL**
  (person walking between parked cars — correct direction). R3 FAIL (mask
  bottom jitter, the known POC mode). R1 NO_DATA (no zone drawn — the POC's
  KRTD-world zone WKT is in a different frame than the MoGe floor frame and
  is deliberately not reused).
- Spend: 0 SAM calls, 3 MoGe keyframes.

## Run 2 — school.G299 live (~$0.88)

`runs/video-school-g299`, 44 frames @1 fps from 230 s (the busiest activity
window per the MEVA annotations, frames 6900–8190), labels person/car.

- SAM: **88/88 live calls, zero failures** ($0.88).
- Floor: fitted, camera height 3.322 m, spread 0.051 m, inliers 0.69.
- Tracks: 15 person tracks (school crowd; short fragments from occlusion).
  R2 **NO_DATA — honest**: no car present in the sampled window. R3 FAIL:
  median frame speed 1.08 m/s (walking) with 9 FAIL frames at 2.9–5.1 m/s
  around frames 7200–7590 — either genuine running (school campus) or
  ID-switch jitter; the tier cannot distinguish and reports the band.
- Overall FAIL is the conservative worst-of-rules roll-up.
