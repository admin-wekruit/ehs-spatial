## Design Context

Panoptes serves engineers and EHS reviewers who start with photographs of one workcell. The user explicitly wants one complete report containing the original photo, object evidence, spatial reconstruction, CAD and plan, generated assets including Blender, and policy findings. Reading is the first step; correcting missed objects and reviewing reasons with an Agent is the second step in the same report. Precise modeling remains an explicit secondary action.

Use Lucida's large scene demonstration and connected evidence as the reference. Preserve every existing evidence artifact and its run, camera, coordinate and revision identity. Avoid empty editing panels as the landing experience, repeated playgrounds, raw process logs without outcomes, and scattering one workcell across separate product pages. Chinese and English share the same state. On phones, retain all views with a view switcher.

Use a compact, three-column report interaction area: all object records are always accessible in a searchable left rail; four linked photo, 3D, CAD and plan panes occupy the center; selected-object measurements, evidence and exact safety findings occupy the right rail. Center panes support four-view, single-view and fullscreen modes without resetting selection or camera. Keep the title and navigation compact so the scene is visible immediately. Below this area retain the complete understanding, safety, assets, Blender and historical evidence sections with progressive disclosure. Existing typefaces and components are reused. Colors should support evidence and selection rather than decorate the page.
